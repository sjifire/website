  The Public is notified that the Board of Commissioners for SJC Fire Protection District 3 has adopted Resolution No. 26-07 effective July 21, 2026.

Resolution No. 26-07 repeals and replaces prior regulations and establishes updated terms and conditions for the issuance and use of burn permits within the boundaries of SJC Fire Protection District No. 3.
