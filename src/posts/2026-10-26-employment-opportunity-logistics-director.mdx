## Position Details

* **Position:** Logistics Director
* **Department:** Administration
* **Reports To:** Fire Chief
* **FLSA Status:** Exempt
* **Work Schedule:** 40-hour work week
* **Anticipated Start Date:** January 2027

## Salary and Benefits

* **Starting Salary:** $114,000 annually
* Employer-paid health, dental, and vision insurance for eligible employees and eligible family members
* Washington PERS retirement participation
* 457(b) supplemental retirement plan
* HRA-VEBA matching contribution
* Paid vacation, sick leave, and holidays
* District-paid professional development and CDL endorsement training
* Moving expense reimbursement

## Position Summary

The Logistics Director is a key leadership position responsible for planning, organizing, directing, and evaluating all district logistics functions including fleet, facilities, procurement, contracts, inventory management, asset accountability, volunteer support programs, and logistical readiness.

**Supervises:** Quartermaster; Fleet Technician; Volunteer Technicians; other personnel when performing maintenance and repair activities.

**Collaborates With:** Finance Officer, Battalion Chief of Emergency Services, Battalion Chief of Training, Health and Safety, ITT Administrator, vendors, contractors, and stakeholders.

## Essential Duties and Responsibilities

* Provide leadership and oversight of district logistics operations.
* Oversee fleet, apparatus, equipment, and facility maintenance programs.
* Proficiently utilize Microsoft 365, Outlook, and district software systems.
* Develop assigned bids, specifications, contracts, and procurement documents.
* Administer logistics budgets, cost analyses, and expenditure tracking.
* Supervise contractors, vendors, and maintenance personnel.
* Manage inventory and asset accountability systems.
* Support organizational readiness.
* Participate in and perform routine fleet and facilities maintenance and repair work as necessary.

## Minimum Qualifications

* High school diploma or GED.
* Valid Washington State Driver License.
* Ability to obtain and maintain required Commercial Driver endorsement within one year of hire.
* Experience with budgeting, procurement, expense tracking, and contract administration.
* Ability to physically perform fleet and facility maintenance work.

## Preferred Knowledge, Skills and Abilities

* Facilities repair, maintenance and construction.
* EVT, ASE, and/or diesel and equipment mechanic.
* Strong organizational and planning skills.
* Excellent verbal and written communication abilities.
* Commitment to continuing professional development and organizational culture.
* Experience working with volunteers.

## Leadership Expectations

* Commitment to the District mission, values, culture, and service philosophy.
* Acts as a collaborative administrative leader and steward of public resources.
