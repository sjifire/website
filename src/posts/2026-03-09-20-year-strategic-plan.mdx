To date, Board of Fire Protection District 3 Commissioners have reviewed the draft Plan and are seeking community involvement prior to its adoption at its next regular meeting.

The public and our first responder partners are invited to review and provide written comments by Friday, March 13, 2026 or verbal comments at the regular Fire Commission meeting on Tuesday, March 17, 2026.

Written comments on the Plan can be forwarded to Chief Monin at [chief@sjifire.org](mailto\:chief@sjifire.org).

For more information or to get involved, contact SJIF\&R through [www.sjifire.org](http://www.sjifire.org) or [www.facebook.com/SJIFire](http://www.facebook.com/SJIFire).
