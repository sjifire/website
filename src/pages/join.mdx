## Volunteer Opportunities

APPLICATIONS ARE **<mark style={{ backgroundColor: "#FEF08A" }}>OPEN</mark>** for our upcoming **2027** academy.

Fill out an application and you will be contacted with the next steps.

**Who?** You! We are looking for individuals who are 18+ years of age who want to serve their neighbors, friends and families by becoming qualified structural and wildland firefighters.

**Why?** Becoming a part of our department welcomes you into a group of people committed to supporting each other and members of this wonderful community we call home.  Volunteering helps to keep your community safe, provides quicker response times, and even reduces homeowner insurance costs.

**What?** Firefighters are trained to respond to all sorts of hazards. You learn about fire behavior, fire suppression, structure search and rescue techniques, teamwork, First Aid / CPR, and personal and team safety.

IFSAC Firefighter 1 certification will be available. Learn more about IFSAC certifications here: [https://wsp.wa.gov/fire-service-certification/](https://wsp.wa.gov/fire-service-certification/)

Recruits will also have the opportunity to become wildand firefighters and obtain a “Red Card” or Incident Qualification Card. Learn more about Wildland training here: [https://www.nwcg.gov/nwcg-training](https://www.nwcg.gov/nwcg-training)

**How?** The process starts with filling out an application. You will be contacted with times to schedule an interview, physical agility test and a fitting for gear and uniform. A background check is also conducted and based on results, an invitation to join is offered.

A physical exam, paid for by the Department will be conducted to determine fitness, cardiac, and respiratory health before the start of the training.

### Academy Timeline:

Applications for the 2027 Academy are due October 10, 2026 - please submit an application if you are interested in Wildland only

Onboarding takes place at the end of 2026 with 2 or 3 days of testing and prerequisites.

Class syllabus and materials will be provided in December as well as a First Aid and CPR class.

The 2027 training class is split into two sections:

* Structural Firefighting Academy: Jan-April with IFSAC FF1 testing.
* Wildland Firefighting Academy: April

**Training days will be Saturdays and Sundays between 8:00AM and 5:00PM**

Feel free to contact our Training Officer at [training@sjifire.org](mailto\:training@sjifire.org) with any questions.  Or come by Headquarters and meet the crews.
