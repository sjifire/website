## Forms & Service Requests

### Prevention Services

Request inspections, KnoxBox installations, fire alarm checks, fire extinguisher training, station tours, or public education programs.

[Request Prevention Services](https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=j4QiQRfDZ0KcB3xQQVDRvVafkl0BixBCpOYQ4VdsPFZUMlgxSEtST0NYMVVWV0FUMTY3SDVISFUwVi4u)

### Volunteer Application

Join San Juan Island Fire & Rescue as a volunteer firefighter or brigade member.

[Submit Volunteer Application](https://forms.office.com/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=j4QiQRfDZ0KcB3xQQVDRva2d14yWAXNFl-Q6Prz2fIRUOFNSWEYyUEsxS0dFNVpITkdEWFg3RklHWCQlQCN0PWcu&route=shorturl)

### Burn Permits

Burn permits are issued by San Juan County:

- **Residential:** [Apply at San Juan County](https://www.sanjuanco.com/1161/Apply-for-Burn-Permits)
- **Commercial:** [Request Inspection](https://www.sanjuanco.com/FormCenter/Fire-Marshal-12/Request-an-Inspection-55)
