Locked doors can delay emergency response. A [KnoxBox](https://www.knoxbox.com) is a high-security, UL-listed key safe that allows first responders to quickly enter your home, business, or gate without forced entry.

Each KnoxBox is keyed specifically for San Juan Island Fire & Rescue—only we have access.

## How to Order

1. Visit the [KnoxBox Products](https://www.knoxbox.com/Products) page
2. Select the red “BUY” option located at the top right of the home page
3. “Your Location” is **Washington State**, and “Local fire department/agency” is **San Juan Co Fire Dist #3**
4. Choose your product: select "Residential Use ONLY - HomeBoxes" for a 1 or 2 key home box, or  “Commercial Knox Products” option for businesses, multi-key needs, or gate boxes.
5. Complete your order—we'll be notified automatically and will contact you if needed
6. When your KnoxBox arrives, mount it and submit a Knox Box Key Install request on our Prevention Services Request form, or call us at **(360) 378-5334** to schedule a visit to finalize setup.
